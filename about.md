# About

I'm Jiyoung, a Supply Chain Management student at York University in Toronto. I'm Korean, I run on financiers and spreadsheets, and I'm moving toward security governance, risk and compliance.

I got here sideways. I found out about co-op, scholarships and exchange programs too late and didn't know who to ask, so I built [SEED Toronto](https://seedtoronto.org) to fix that for the next person. Building it meant writing a privacy policy, deciding who gets admin access, and figuring out how long I'm allowed to keep someone's date of birth. Running SOBOK, my bakery, meant reading food regulations, tax thresholds and transit bylaws before selling a single box.

Somewhere in there I realised the part I actually liked was the rules: how you design a system so it keeps working when real people, real money and real data go through it. That's GRC, and I want to do it properly.

**Now:** studying for CompTIA Security+ (exam planned January 2027), writing here as I go, and looking for 2027 co-op and internship roles in security risk, third-party risk, or operations with a compliance angle.

**Before:** fulfillment operations at Coupang, private dining at Lotte's Signiel, event planning lead at York's Korean Student Association. I also did a summer at Yonsei on operations, entrepreneurship and machine learning.
