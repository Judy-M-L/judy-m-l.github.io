# About

I'm Jiyoung, a Supply Chain Management student at York University in Toronto. I'm Korean, and I run on financiers and spreadsheets.

I got here sideways. I found out about co-op, scholarships and exchange programs too late and didn't know who to ask, so I built [SEED Toronto](https://seedtoronto.org) to fix that for the next person. Running SOBOK, my bakery, meant building a cost sheet, tracking ingredient inventory, and reading food regulations before selling a single box.

**Now:** working through the ASCM Supply Chain Technology Certificate (through January 2027), learning SQL basics, keeping study notes here as I go, and looking for 2027 co-op and internship roles in supply chain and operations.

**Before:** part-time shifts at a Coupang fulfillment centre, event planning lead at York's Korean Student Association. I also did a summer at Yonsei on operations, entrepreneurship and machine learning.
